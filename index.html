The doubled line — PIE

Personal Intent Engine: press a global hotkey in any application, speak naturally, and a structured, production-ready prompt lands at your cursor. Transcription, correction, and intent extraction all happen on this side of the network. Audio never leaves the machine.

  • StackRust · Tauri 2 · Svelte 5. Ships as a desktop app, a CLI, and a Rust crate.
  • Speechwhisper.cpp with Metal acceleration, gated by Silero voice-activity detection.
  • CorrectorLayered pronunciation correction for developer jargon ("next jazz" → Next.js): built-in dictionary, personal vocabulary, and an opt-in AI pass, with a context-gated phonetic tier so ordinary words are never falsely corrected.
  • TestingIntegration suite with a mock-LLM harness, VAD tests, and a corrector-pipeline suite; intent-extraction results documented in-repo.
  • ReleaseSigned builds, one-line installer, Homebrew tap, local SQLite history.
